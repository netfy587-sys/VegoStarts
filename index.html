How to start your first windowsill garden

👩‍🌾By the VegoStarts garden team · 8 min read · Beginner
Contents1. Find the light2. Pots & drainage3. Soil4. Easy plants5. Watering6. Pests7. Harvest

Growing food at home is easier than most people think. You do not need a big garden, only a bright spot, a few pots and a little curiosity. Start Today – Thank Yourself Tomorrow.

1. Find the light ☀️

Most herbs and vegetables like about four to six hours of sunlight. Watch your window for a day and note where the sun falls. A south- or west-facing window is often brightest in India, but any bright spot can work for leafy greens.

2. Choose pots with drainage 🪴

Every pot needs holes at the bottom. Without them, water collects and roots can rot. Old buckets, tubs and recycled containers work well when you poke drainage holes and place a tray underneath.

Small changes create big impacts.

3. Use light, healthy soil 🌍

Garden soil can become heavy in pots. A simple mix is potting soil or cocopeat blended with compost and a little sand or perlite, so water can drain while nutrients stay available.

4. Pick easy plants 🌿

Beginners do well with mint, coriander, basil, spring onions, lettuce, spinach and radish. They grow quickly, so you see results and stay motivated. Chillies and tomatoes come next when you have brighter light.

5. Water with the finger test 💧

Push a finger about an inch into the soil. If it feels dry, water slowly until it drips from the bottom. If it feels damp, wait a day. Morning is usually the best time, and it is better to water deeply less often than a little every hour.

6. Keep pests away, gently 🐞

Check leaves once a week. Remove small pests by hand or with a gentle water spray. A diluted neem-based spray is a common natural option; always follow the label and test on one leaf first.

7. Harvest and enjoy 🥗

Pick outer leaves and herb tips often. Regular harvesting encourages fresh growth. Rinse everything well before eating and enjoy the taste of something you grew yourself.

Regrow from kitchen scraps ♻️

Zero-cost gardening. Everything organized in three easy steps.

Save the base

Keep the white root end of spring onions or the base of a lettuce head.

Place in shallow water

Stand it in a glass on a bright sill and change the water every day or two.

Watch it regrow

New green shoots often appear in a few days. Move to soil for stronger growth.

💡 Did you know?

Compost from vegetable peels, tea leaves and dry leaves turns into dark, crumbly soil food. Avoid meat, oil and dairy in a home compost bin.

Growing through the seasons 🍂

General ideas for most of India. Check local weather before sowing.

❄️

Winter (Oct–Feb)

Coriander, spinach, lettuce, radish, peas and methi enjoy the cooler weather.

☀️

Summer (Mar–Jun)

Chilli, okra, basil and tulsi like warmth. Water more often and give afternoon shade if it is very hot.

🌧️

Monsoon (Jul–Sep)

Good for mint, spinach and beans. Make sure pots drain well and are not sitting in water.

VegoStarts FAQ ❓

Short, honest answers.

What is VegoStarts?

VegoStarts is an Online Adventures Platform, a blog where you can read and learn about growing herbs, greens and vegetables at home.

Is VegoStarts a shop?

No. It is an informational blog with guides and tips. It does not sell products.

I have no garden. Can I still grow food?

Yes. A sunny windowsill, balcony or even a bright kitchen shelf is enough for herbs and microgreens.

Are all plants safe to eat?

No. Only eat plants you can identify as edible, wash them well and keep pesticides away from anything you plan to harvest. If you are unsure, ask a local nursery or expert.
